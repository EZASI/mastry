Product Pricing

MASTRY is not yet available for purchase. Prices (inclusive of Japanese consumption tax) will be clearly displayed on each product page at the time of sales launch. To be notified at launch, please register for early access on our home page.

Additional Costs

The following costs may apply in addition to the product price and will be disclosed in full at launch:

  • Shipping fees within Japan (shown at checkout, based on delivery address and quantity)
  • Consumption tax (8%, the reduced rate for food and beverages) — included in all displayed prices
  • Any payment-processing or bank-transfer fees charged by the customer's financial institution

No catalogue or membership fees are charged.

Payment Method

Accepted payment methods will be confirmed and displayed at launch. We expect to support major credit cards and other common online payment options.

Payment Timing

Payment timing will be confirmed at launch and displayed clearly before the customer completes checkout. For card payments, the charge is generally processed at the time of order confirmation.

Delivery Timing

Estimated delivery times will be published at launch. We plan to dispatch orders within 2–3 business days of order and payment confirmation. MASTRY ships within Japan only.

Returns and Exchanges

As MASTRY is a food product, we cannot accept returns or exchanges for personal preference or ordering error.

If the product arrives damaged or leaking, the wrong item is delivered, or there is a manufacturing defect, please contact us within 7 days of delivery. The steps are on our shipping and returns page.

For any pre-launch enquiries about future terms, please contact us at hello@mastry.jp.

Press, Wholesale & Partnerships

Media and press enquiries, wholesale or distribution enquiries, and partnership requests should be submitted via the relevant category on our contact form.
